How it works

  • You are a king and move one square at a time, diagonals included.
  • Drop a piece where you stand. One and a half seconds later it attacks every square it could capture on, the way that piece moves in chess. A rook sweeps lines, a bishop diagonals, a knight jumps, a pawn hits the two diagonal squares ahead. Rubble, other pieces and kings stop sliding pieces.
  • A king on a hit square is captured and loses. A piece in a blast goes off at once. You always drop your best piece: queen, rook, bishop, knight, then pawn. Pawns never run out, and every other piece comes back to you once it has gone off.
  • Blow up rubble to uncover pieces lying on the ground. Walk over one to pick it up. Blasts destroy pieces that are still lying there.
  • There is no warning where a piece will hit, you read the piece, like reading a bomb's range in Bomberman. The clock is two minutes, then it is a draw.

Bomber-Chess is my mash-up of Bomberman and chess. You play a king on a chess board, the bombs are chess pieces, and each one blows up exactly the squares it attacks. Blow up the rubble to find bigger pieces, then catch the other king in a blast.

Nothing leaves your browser. This is an early prototype: two players on one keyboard or against a simple bot, with phone to phone play planned.

Provided free of charge, without tracking. For the love of the game.
